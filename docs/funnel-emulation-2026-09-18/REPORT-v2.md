## Remaining blockers

- 1. Silence after a handoff (5 of 6 walkers, Anna rated it a blocker): once Maya is paused, a Telegram client's next message gets no holding reply and the owner gets no alert, so booking requests and clinical follow-ups are lost. Fix: send the client a holding notice in their language and alert the owner on each new inbound message (throttled), or hand back to Maya automatically after N minutes.
- 2. The owner cannot see or protect her calendar (critic: blocker; #1 reason for Jessica and Marc; Priya and Olena too): there is no bookings or week view linked from the app and no busy-time blocking, and preview test bookings hold real slots and count as revenue. Calendar sync is a +$29 add-on connected by hand, not in the trial, with no Jane App. So owners won't share the link with real clients, and the trial shows no value.
- 3. Payment happens later (all 6): step 7 files a request and the invoice from INNOVA CONSULT LTD arrives by email in 1 business day. The company name is not on signup or step 7, the owner gets no confirmation, and the wording about the trial end date is confusing. Needs Stripe keys (the code path exists) or at least a card link on the screen and an automatic confirmation.
- 4. Demo dashboard still reachable (Jessica, Anna, critic): Luminous Core / Sarah J. / Apr 17 / made-up trends, revenue that counts test and cancelled bookings, and '+$15' parsed as a price. Hide it for self-serve tenants or rebuild it on real data.
- 5. Maya slips that cost money: impossible times offered in free text (after closing, staff on a day off or for a service they don't do); 'Friday' said on a Friday read as today; a year of 2024 once; loops on 'yes, but <question>' and in RU/FR multi-question threads; a consult turned into another service after Yes (medspa, harmful); invented policies (bottom lashes, discounts); the owner's fill rule dropped; a price question skipped.
- 6. Late cancel and deposit handling: the fee is not mentioned before confirming; late cancels and reschedules are not flagged to the owner; there is no deposit tracking while the slot is held; the client hears 'booked' but also 'confirmed once the deposit is received'.
- 7. Text the owner typed is sent untranslated: policy, staff and service names reach clients in the owner's language, and French clients see English service names (a problem for Quebec).
- 8. Preview: it locks after one handoff (in another language), the first reply took about 90 s once, and a reload returns to step 1.
- 9. Medspa/Pro trust: data on a VPS in Europe, no DPA, no BC PIPA mention, no named privacy officer; 'Consultation only' erases 'from $X'; the medspa type does not create a consultation service; the manager is bookable for injectables by default.
- 10. Price objections from channels: Instagram DM (+$29) is where clients write, so the real price for a small salon is $108-137 + tax, against the headline 'from $39'. The chat link is a long technical URL with no short or vanity link.
